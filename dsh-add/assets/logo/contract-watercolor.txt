WATERCOLOUR LOGO. Medium is the whole point: this must read as a real watercolour painting on cold-press paper, not as flat cel-shaded anime art with a paper texture laid over it.

MEDIUM - non-negotiable:
- Pigment granulation, wet-on-wet bleeds and soft feathered edges. Where two colours meet they bloom into each other; edges are broken and irregular, never a hard continuous outline.
- Colour is transparent and luminous: white paper shows through the washes. Tints sit in overlapping layers instead of one flat fill.
- Loose dry-brush strokes on the hair and skirt; visible brush texture; a few pigment spatters.
- The four sides of the image fade into unpainted white paper.
- Paper: cold-press watercolour paper tooth, visible in the unpainted areas and under the washes.

COLOUR - low saturation, as requested:
- Muted, washed-out and gentle: desaturated dusty navy and dusty blue for the hair, with soft pale-cyan tips; off-white and pale grey for the frills and apron; a single restrained antique-gold accent.
- No neon, no high-chroma saturated blue, no glossy highlights, no strong contrast. Aim for the value range of a light watercolour sketch.

AUTHORITY - character design, unchanged by the medium: DeepSeek whale-chan. Deep-sea navy blue long hair grading to curly cyan tips, a wide round face with large deep-blue eyes, small nose, fixed blush, a pair of side whale-fin ears, a single forward-curving ahoge, a naturally attached whale tail, and her navy-and-white maid dress with a navy neck ribbon holding a sapphire, a white frilled headdress, a white frilled apron and collar, dark-gold piping, and white socks.

PROPORTION - chibi / Q-version, about 3.5 heads tall, big head and small body. Never a tall realistic figure.

COMPOSITION - a clean logo emblem, and nothing else in the frame:
- Only the chibi whale-chan, seen from the front, whole body inside the frame.
- She holds ONE rounded-square plugin cartridge in both hands in front of her: a small navy face with a thin dark-gold border, two or three little gold contact pins along one edge, and a tiny white whale-tail glyph on its face. That cartridge is the only object besides herself.
- ABSOLUTELY NO other floating objects: no additional cartridges or blocks anywhere in the background, no duplicates, no sparkles, no stars, no confetti, no scattered particles other than a few faint pigment spatters from the watercolour technique itself.
- Plain unpainted white paper background. No scenery, no horizon, no ground shadow, no framing border, no vignette.

EXCLUSIONS: not 3D render, not photograph, not digital airbrush, not cel-shaded, not flat vector fill, not realistic proportions, no tall adult figure, no extra limbs, no second character, no text, no letters, no numbers, no signature, no watermark, no logo lockup.
